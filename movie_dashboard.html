Release Timing Matters

The month a film releases significantly impacts its revenue. Summer and holiday windows dominate.

+247%
Revenue difference: June ($159.8M avg) vs January ($46.1M avg)
  • June leads all months at $159.8M average revenue
  • May close second ($139.9M) — summer blockbuster season
  • November ($126.5M) benefits from Thanksgiving audiences
  • January is the weakest month — studios dump low-confidence films
⭐

Quality vs Popularity

Critical acclaim and commercial success don't always align by genre.

6.5
Animation median rating — highest of all genres
  • Drama consistently scores highest (median 6.3/10)
  • Horror has lowest median rating (5.4) but 2nd best ROI
  • Animation: best of both — high ratings AND high ROI
  • Action earns most total revenue but mediocre ratings (5.9)

User Rating Behavior

Analysis of 100K+ user ratings reveals strong positive bias in movie scoring patterns.

  • 4.0/5.0 is the single most common rating (27,517 times)
  • 3.0 follows as 2nd most common — users tend to be generous
  • Scores below 2.0 are relatively rare (<5% of all ratings)
  • Average user rating: 3.5 / 5.0 across all movies
🌍

Franchise Power

Movies belonging to a collection/franchise earn on average 3.2× more revenue than standalone films. Sequels consistently outperform their predecessor budgets.

🎭

Genre Overlap Premium

Movies spanning Action+Adventure+Sci-Fi generate the highest combined revenue. The top 1% of earners almost all combine 3+ popular genres, creating broad audience appeal.

🏆

The 100M Club

Only 18% of movies with known budgets cross the $100M revenue threshold. Yet these films account for over 72% of total worldwide box office receipts in the dataset.
